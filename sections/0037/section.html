A bar that stays on the phone.

Call, text, and book, fixed to the bottom of a small screen. On a wide screen the same three actions sit in the page and do not cover it. The number is a placeholder.

Pinned on a small screen

Under 48rem the bar locks to the bottom of the viewport. Body padding, plus the safe-area inset, is the same height as the bar, so the last line of the page can scroll clear of the buttons.

In the page on a desktop

At 48rem and up the bar is no longer fixed. It becomes a row of links under the introduction. Resize this window to see both.

The inset under the buttons

On an iPhone the bar grows by the home-indicator inset, and the body padding grows with it. The frame on a wide screen shows that inset at 22px so the gap is visible without a phone.

Three real links

Call is a tel link. Text is an sms link. Book is an ordinary link to a target on this page. Each one is the full width of its third of the bar, and taller than 44px.

Book a visit

This is an empty target for the Book link. Point that link at the real scheduler. Nothing on this page books a job. Placeholder number: (555) 010-0137.
